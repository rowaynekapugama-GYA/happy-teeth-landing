A confident smile

Straighter teeth, without a mouth full of metal

Crowded, gapped or crooked teeth can hold you back from smiling freely — and they can be harder to clean, too. Clear aligners are a series of custom-made, nearly invisible trays that gradually move your teeth into their ideal position, without brackets or wires.

Why patients choose aligners

  • ✓ Nearly invisible — most people won't notice them
  • ✓ Removable, so you eat, brush and floss normally
  • ✓ Smooth plastic, no brackets or wires to irritate
  • ✓ Fewer, shorter check-up visits than braces
Suits most cases

What aligners can treat

  • ✓ Crowded or overlapping teeth
  • ✓ Gaps between teeth
  • ✓ Many bite concerns (overbite, underbite, crossbite)
  • ✓ Relapse after braces earlier in life

Good to know

Common questions

Am I suitable for clear aligners?
Aligners can treat a wide range of concerns — crowding, gaps and many bite issues — for teens and adults alike. Some complex cases may need a different approach, and the only way to know for certain is a consultation, where your suitability is assessed in person. Your first consultation with us is free.
How do the "from $59 per week" payments work?
Weekly figures are based on treatment financed through a MediPay payment plan, subject to MediPay's terms, approval and eligibility criteria. Your exact repayment depends on your treatment plan, deposit and term — we'll give you the actual numbers in writing at your consultation before you decide anything.
How long does aligner treatment take?
It depends on how far your teeth need to move. Minor corrections can take a few months, while more involved cases take longer. Your personalised plan will show your expected timeline — and you'll often see your projected result digitally before you begin.
Do aligners hurt?
You may feel gentle pressure for a day or two when you change to a new tray — that's a sign your teeth are moving. Most people find aligners far more comfortable than braces, with no brackets or wires to irritate your cheeks and lips.
Can I eat and drink normally?
Yes — that's one of the biggest advantages. You simply remove your aligners to eat and drink (anything other than water), brush, and pop them back in. No food restrictions like braces.
Will people notice I'm wearing them?
Clear aligners are made from thin, transparent material that sits snugly over your teeth. Most people won't notice you're wearing them, even up close — which is why they're so popular with adults and teens.
